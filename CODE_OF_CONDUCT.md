# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Veyra project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing respect and consideration for others.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than attacking people.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language or actions.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or deliberately insulting comments.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or sexually explicit language.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of project discussions or collaboration.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in Veyra project spaces, including repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed channels.

It also applies when an individual is publicly representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate it.

Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, contact the project maintainers privately:

Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible. Please do not post details of a conduct report publicly.

For suspected security vulnerabilities, follow the private reporting instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may use the following responses depending on the severity and circumstances of a violation:

1. **Correction** — A private or public reminder that the behavior is inappropriate, with a request to stop.
2. **Warning** — A warning that continued behavior may result in a temporary or permanent restriction.
3. **Temporary restriction** — A temporary restriction from participating in project spaces.
4. **Permanent restriction** — A permanent restriction from participating in project spaces.

Maintainers may skip steps when a serious violation requires immediate action.
